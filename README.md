Overview
This project presents an end-to-end HR analytics solution designed to analyze employee attrition patterns and uncover key factors influencing workforce retention. The dashboard enables data-driven decision-making by providing actionable insights into employee behavior, compensation impact, and departmental trends.
Business Objective
Organizations often struggle to understand:
Why employees leave
Which departments face high attrition
How salary and experience influence retention
This project addresses these challenges through interactive analytics and insight generation.

Dashboard Features

Job Role-wise Attrition
Gender Distribution

Key Insights
Employees with 0–2 years experience show the highest attrition (~36%)
Attrition decreases significantly for employees with higher tenure (~8% for 10+ years)
Low salary band has the highest attrition (~29%), indicating compensation impact
Sales and HR departments show higher attrition rates compared to others
Specific roles like Laboratory Technician and Sales Executive contribute heavily to exits
Business Recommendations
Improve onboarding and engagement for early-stage employees
Optimize salary structures for lower pay bands
Focus retention strategies on high-risk departments
Provide career growth and development opportunities
Continuously monitor workforce trends using analytics
